# M7.5 — Human scale: specification

Milestone M7.5 of `docs/gcity-design.md` §16, **proposed as a new milestone** between the
route graph (M7) and look and feel (M8). Written before implementation (standards §2.1,
§10.2). Status: **approved** as written (CEOGG, 2026-10-01: "approve the spec"), its six
decisions included; proposed by Claude Code the same day. **Amended** the same day with
claims 14–16, the creator tool (CEOGG: "yes add the creator tool and go").

**Why this milestone exists.** CEOGG's first hand-played Deck run (2026-10-01, G7 gate §7)
found the player "much larger than the buildings". It is: a build cell is 1 m on every
side, every wall piece is one cell high, and the player is drawn 1.8 m tall. In the sim a
person is a point at its feet, and every rule — standing, stepping, climbing, sight,
cover — asks about the one cell the feet are in. So a one-cell row behaves like a whole
storey in the sim, while the view draws it waist-high. M8 would put 1.8 m rigged people
next to those walls. CEOGG chose (G7 gate §6 item 4, option (a)) to fix the scale in the
sim first, so the look is built once on the scale the game actually has.

**Preconditions.** G7 is signed. This spec is approved. ADR-003 (volumetric 1 m terrain)
stands: this milestone keeps the 1 m cell and changes what fits in it.

**The claim of the milestone.** A person is **two cells tall** and a storey is **three
cells**, in the sim and in the content, and nothing behaves as if a person still fits in
one cell. Every rule that asks where a body can be asks about both of its cells; sight
runs between eyes, not feet; every site, route and fixture is rebuilt at the new scale;
and the client draws what the sim now means, so a 1.8 m capsule stands in a 3 m room
under a 2 m door.

**What this milestone is not.** It is not M8: no models, materials, sound or HUD. It is
not a crouch, prone or half-cover system (a posture is a new system, recorded below for
later). It does not teach agents to path between storeys (G6 debt, still open; see
Out of scope). It does not change the cell size: cells stay 1 000 mm, the terrain stays
1 m voxels, and every millimetre constant keeps its meaning.

**Size.** One rule (the body) applied across every system that places or moves an actor,
one change to sight, one to structural support, and a re-authoring of all content with a
vertical extent. It touches many `sim/` modules, so CLAUDE.md's "one system per session"
orders the work: one claim per session, in the order below, the suite green between
them.

## Decisions this spec takes

Each is the option that keeps the sim's existing meaning and changes the least.
Approved with the spec, 2026-10-01.

1. **Cells stay 1 000 mm.** A person is the feet cell and the cell above it (2 m of
   room); a storey is three cells floor to floor (3 m), so a room has one cell of
   headroom over a person. Shrinking the cell instead would move every millimetre value
   in the sim and content and the terrain's rounding, for the same result.
2. **Eyes at 1 600 mm, the body's centre at 1 000 mm.** Sight runs from the observer's
   eyes to the contact's eyes and to its centre; either clear line sees. A shot needs a
   clear line from the shooter's eyes to the target's centre.
3. **An opening is a column of faces.** A door is two stacked door faces (2 m); a
   window is a wall face with a window face above it (a sill at 1 m). A body crosses a
   face boundary only where both of its rows are passable. No new piece concept: the
   site files stack faces, as walls already stack.
4. **Support counts horizontal hops only.** A piece standing on a supported piece
   directly below it is supported at the same depth; `max_span` limits overhangs, not
   height. Without this a three-cell wall on a roof runs out of span (Cold Storage's
   roof is at depth 3 today, concrete's span is 2).
5. **Falls are measured in metres.** A drop of up to 3 m (one storey) is free; each
   metre beyond costs `fall_damage_per_level`, re-tuned so a two-storey fall does what a
   two-level fall did at M6.
6. **Saves move to schema 3 and a schema-2 save is refused** with a clear message. No
   player holds a save yet; a migration would be code for nobody.

## Claims

### The body (`sim/agents/`, `sim/world/`)

1. **Both body cells, everywhere.** `MovementSystem` steps, level changes, climbs and
   auto-steps require both body cells free of ground and cell pieces, and the faces
   crossed passable in both rows; falling, standing and the gate set-down are unchanged
   at the feet. The same body test is the only one used by `PathingSystem.can_step`,
   `SquadSystem._outside_cell`, `RaidTokenSystem` tokens, `HydrationSystem` set-down and
   `Regions` gate set-down: one function, called from all of them, so they cannot
   disagree.
2. **The body invariant.** Property, 10 000 generated build/move/fall sequences: no
   actor ever has ground or a cell piece in either body cell, and no actor ever crosses a
   face boundary where either row is impassable. A one-cell gap admits no one.

### Sight and cover (`sim/agents/perception_system.gd`, stance, combat)

3. **Eyes, not feet.** `can_see`/`can_target` walk from eyes to eyes and eyes to centre
   (decision 2) with the existing grid walk, both directions as now. A one-cell wall or
   crate no longer blocks sight over it; a two-cell wall does. Stance's `in_cover` asks
   the same lines. Metamorphic: raising a wall by a row never makes a hidden contact
   visible; removing a row never makes a visible one hidden.
4. **Reach from the body.** Reach checks (corpses, terminals, digging) measure from the
   body's centre, with their content numbers unchanged.

### Building (`sim/land/build_system.gd`, content)

5. **Support by column** (decision 4), with a property: stacking a supported column
   higher never makes a piece under it unsupported, and the M3 partition invariants hold
   unchanged over 10 000 build/destroy sequences.
6. **Sites at human scale.** `tools/make_sites.gd` rebuilds `m4_test_building` as one
   three-cell storey with two-cell doors and sill windows, and `cold_storage` as a slab
   and two three-cell storeys, its ladders, flights, hatches, grate and tunnel kept in
   the same plan and order; patrol routes, spawns, terminals and parcel bounds move with
   them. The three M6 routes are re-proved completable by their fixtures (claim 9).
7. **Falls in metres** (decision 5), in the combat profiles.

### The world (`sim/world/`)

8. **The wilds fit a person.** Road clearance and site levelling are checked against a
   two-cell body and a three-cell storey (levelling clears to the site's top plus one
   storey); the one-cell terrain step stays a step. The M7 connectivity property is
   re-run over 10 000 seeds with the body rule in.

### The creator tool (`client/`, `tools/`) — amendment, 2026-10-01

Until now a place in the game could only be written as code (`tools/make_sites.gd`), so
only Claude Code could make one. This milestone rebuilds every site anyway; the tool
makes the next ones something CEOGG builds on the Deck.

14. **Build a site in the game.** `--create` opens a flat, open lot the player owns, with
    every `build_piece` in the palette and two markers (a guard post, a terminal). A
    cursor cell is moved with the D-pad (relative to the camera) and up and down a level
    with the bumpers; A places the selected piece on the cursor (on the face toward the
    camera for wall-like pieces, X turns it), B removes what is under the cursor, Y picks
    the next piece. Everything goes through `SimRoot.submit` like any other build, so
    support, rights and the body rule judge it: a site the tool makes is valid because the
    sim placed it. Controller only, no keyboard (CLAUDE.md §10), and the run log records
    every placement.
15. **Save and reopen.** The device's menu saves the lot as a site file in the existing
    schema (`content/site`, version 1): pieces relative to the lowest corner in placement
    order, guard posts as spawns facing the way they were placed, terminals; to
    `user://sites/<id>.json`, the id from the date and time. A saved or shipped site can
    be opened in `--create` to keep working on it. Round-trip property: for 1 000 random
    builds, saving, then raising the file with `site.raise` on an empty lot, gives the
    same pieces in the same cells with the same facings.
16. **Into the game.** `tools/import_site.py <file> <id> [title]` validates the file
    against the schema (the same validator CI runs), renames it, and writes
    `content/site/<id>.json`; from there a site can be raised or bound like Cold Storage.
    Fuzz target: the importer refuses malformed, oversized and out-of-range files with a
    message, corpus in the repo. The tool does not touch patrol routes, parcels or kits
    yet: a guard post gets the default `guard_sim` profile and kit (out of scope below).

### Verification

9. **Every fixture re-recorded, every change explained.** All 24 replay fixtures are
   regenerated by their generators (the hand-authored `m3-*` by a new
   `tools/make_m3_fixtures.gd`, so none is edited by hand again), each reproducing twice;
   the gate lists every fixture with why its hash moved. The M6 stealth fixture still
   scores `seen 0, alarms 0, bodies 0, traces 0`.
10. **Saves**: schema 3, round-trip property; a schema-2 file is refused with a message
    naming the version (decision 6), with a test.
11. **The client draws the sim's scale.** The player and guards stand two cells tall in
    three-cell rooms; overlays (awareness bars, sight lines, last-known markers) are
    placed from the actor's floor, not at absolute heights; the camera's eye is the sim's
    eye height. Gate screenshots of the M4 building, Cold Storage and the wilds at the new
    scale.
12. **Mutation score ≥ 75 % on the changed `sim/` files**, both passes reported, as at G7.
13. **Deck run.** `--mission --demo` and `--wilds` on the Deck at 40 fps (1 % low), and
    CEOGG's hand-played notes on whether a person now looks the size of the buildings.

## Out of scope (goes to the debt log if touched)

- **Agents changing storeys.** `PathingSystem` refuses a goal on another level (G6 debt;
  M6 spec claim 2's level change was met by scripted routes). Three-cell storeys make it
  no harder; it stays with the threat director (M9), whose raids need it.
- Crouch, prone and half cover; vaulting; ladders as a separate piece kind.
- Anything M8 owns: models, animation, materials, the HUD.
- Changing the cell size or the terrain resolution (ADR-003).
- In the creator tool: drawing patrol routes, choosing guard profiles and kits, parcels,
  terrain shaping, undo beyond removing a piece, and a shared or online site library.

## Open points

- **Low walls stop being cover.** Under decision 2 a one-cell wall or crate hides
  nothing, because eyes are above it. Today it hides everything. That is correct for a
  standing person and leaves the M4/M6 guards' cover choices to re-tuning by fixture; a
  crouch would bring low cover back and is a later system.
- **The M4 building's interior** grows from 1 m to 3 m high; its `time_to_first_shot`
  (G4) is re-measured and recorded, not re-tuned unless it moves outside G4's band.

## Assumptions to record in the gate

- A person's body is 2 cells for every actor, guards and player alike: there are no
  child, crawling or vehicle actors yet.
- Eye and centre heights are constants in `sim/agents/` (like `REACH_MM`), not content,
  until a second body exists.
