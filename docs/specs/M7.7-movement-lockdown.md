# M7.7 — Movement lockdown: specification

Milestone M7.7 of `docs/gcity-design.md` §16, **proposed as a new milestone** between the
sandbox (M7.6) and look and feel (M8). Written before implementation (standards §2.1,
§10.2). Status: **PROPOSED** by Claude Code, 2026-10-04, from CEOGG's answer to G7.5's
crouching question (gate item 13): *"there should be a milestone coming up, if not create
one for movement lockdown … choose the best point to try to test out crouching. Maybe it
is best that we go over the movement mechanics."* Awaiting CEOGG's approval and the
decisions below.

**Why this milestone exists.** Every way a body moves is still provisional. The player
cannot climb from the controller at all (only scripted demos send `dy`); there is no
crouch, though CEOGG asked for one for stealth (2026-10-03); there is no sprint, so
"run" is just the stick pushed all the way; nothing about how a body moves makes a
sound. Meanwhile M8 is written to animate "idle, walk, run, crouch, climb, aim, fire,
reload, hit, fall, death … from sim state that already exists" (M8 claim 5) with an
empty `sim/` diff, and M9's stealth is tuned against how fast and how visibly the player
moves. Both need the set of moves to be final first. This milestone goes over every
mechanic once, decides it, builds what is missing, binds it to the Deck, and freezes it.

**Why here, and not elsewhere.**
- Not in M7.5: its spec rules posture out ("not a crouch, prone or half-cover system").
- Not in M8: M8 changes nothing under `sim/`, and a crouch is a sim rule (body, eyes,
  speed, what a guard sees).
- After M7.6: the sandbox is how a movement rule gets felt and checked on the Deck
  (spawn a guard behind a low wall, hold the AI, walk past it crouched, draw its sight).
- Before M8 and M9: one hash move for every fixture, once, before M8 re-baselines
  against it; M8 animates a closed list; M9 tunes the threat director against final
  movement instead of re-tuning later.

**Preconditions.** G7.6 is signed. This spec is approved.

**The claim of the milestone.** Every way a body moves is listed in one place, each is a
rule the sim decides through a command, each the player can do from the Deck with one
named button and no keyboard, each is tested and played in Game Mode, and the list is
closed: a move not on it fails a fitness test until a spec adds it.

**What this milestone is not.** Not agents changing storeys or crouching (M9: the AI
uses postures when the threat director needs them). Not hearing footsteps (M9's
signals); this milestone emits the event M9 will listen to. Not animation (M8). Not
swimming, ladders as their own piece kind, or a cover system. No change to raid tokens,
which keep crossing as a standing person (CEOGG, 2026-10-04: as simple as possible).

## The mechanics today (the review CEOGG asked for)

From an audit of `sim/agents/movement_system.gd` and the client, 2026-10-04:

| Mechanic | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | `actor.move {dx, dz}`, capped at the profile's speed (player 6.0 m/s, guard 2.4); speed follows how far the stick is pushed | keep; the player's walk drops to a lower cap (decision 2) |
| Run / sprint | none: full stick is the only "run" | **sprint**: hold a button, faster, more visible (decision 2) |
| Turn | client only for the player; agents through `set_facing` | keep |
| Climb (stairs, ladders) | `dy ±1` through a climbable face; **no player input** | **walk into a flight to climb it**, no button (decision 3) |
| Step up terrain | wilds only, one level | keep |
| Fall | 1 level a tick (40 m/s, no acceleration); 3 m free, then damage per level | keep (decision 5) |
| Crouch | none | **crouch**: a toggle (decision 1) |
| Jump, vault, mantle | none | none in M7.7 (decision 4) |
| Doors, hatches | passable faces; `door_check` wants an access item | keep |
| Body | 2 cells, eyes 1600 mm, centre 1000 mm, per profile | per actor while crouched: 1 cell (claim 2) |
| Noise from moving | none (hearing is `combat.fire` only) | an `actor.stepped` event with a loudness (claim 6) |

## Decisions this spec takes (proposed; each for CEOGG)

1. **Crouch is a toggle on R3** (click the right stick), the one face-adjacent Deck button
   free in normal play. Crouched, a person is one cell tall, eyes 900 mm, centre 500 mm,
   moves at half the walk cap, and adds half the movement term to a guard's exposure
   gain. Standing up where two cells do not fit is refused, and the HUD and run log say
   why ("no room to stand"): never a silent refusal.
2. **Sprint is held on the left trigger** (LT, unbound today). Sprinting moves at the
   current full speed (6.0 m/s); walking with the stick is capped at 3.0 m/s; sprint
   doubles the movement term in exposure and ends crouch. The guard profile keeps 2.4 m/s
   and does not sprint until M9.
3. **Climbing needs no button.** Walking into a stair flight or ladder face climbs it a
   level at a time; walking off the top or down onto a flight descends. The sim rule is
   unchanged (`dy` through a climbable face); the client sends it. Simplest for the Deck,
   and every M6 route that climbs becomes playable by hand.
4. **No jump, vault or mantle in M7.7.** The design doc's §13 transition tags list them,
   but nothing in the content needs them and each is a new body rule. Recorded for a later
   spec if a route wants one.
5. **Falls stay at a constant rate** (one level a tick). Acceleration would move every
   fixture and buys nothing a player sees at 40 ticks a second over a storey or two.

## Claims

### The list (`sim/agents/`)

1. **One closed list.** `MovementSystem.MOVES` names every move (walk, sprint, crouch,
   climb, fall). A fitness test fails when a movement command, a posture or an animation
   name in M8's list is not on it, so a new move needs a spec change.

### Crouch (`sim/agents/`)

2. **A posture per actor.** `actor.posture {actor, posture: "stand" | "crouch"}`; the sim
   refuses standing up where `body_fits(cell, 2)` fails and says why on the bus.
   `body_cells(actor)`, `eye_of` and `centre_of` read the posture, so every rule that
   already asks for a body's height (movement, falls, climbing, sight, shots, the
   creator's guard posts) gets the crouched one without a branch. Saved in the actor
   snapshot (save schema 4).
3. **What a guard sees of a crouched person.** Sight runs to the lower eyes and centre;
   a 1 m wall hides a crouched person from a guard that sees a standing one over it
   (`test_eyes.gd` gains the pair); low cover is back (M7.5 spec, "a crouch would bring
   low cover back").
4. **Crouched movement.** Half speed; a crouched person fits through one-cell gaps a
   standing one does not; climbing works crouched; a fall lands standing.

### Sprint and walk (`sim/agents/`, `content/combat_profile/`)

5. **Sprint.** `actor.move` takes `sprint: true`; the cap and the exposure term follow
   decision 2. Content gains `walk_mm_per_tick` beside `speed_mm_per_tick`.

### Noise (`sim/agents/`)

6. **Steps are events.** Every move a body makes emits `actor.stepped {actor, loudness}`
   (crouched 1, walking 2, sprinting 4), throttled to one a second per actor. No system
   listens yet (M9's hearing); M8's footsteps play from it, so the sound tells the truth.

### The Deck (`client/`)

7. **Every move on its own button.** R3 crouch, LT sprint, climbing by walking (decision
   3). A test pins each to its Deck button like the creator's
   (`test_every_creator_control_is_on_its_own_deck_button`); the HUD shows the posture;
   every refusal is said on the HUD and in the run log.

### Verification

8. **Properties.** 10 000 random walks with random posture and sprint changes: a body is
   never in a solid or in the air, never stands where two cells do not fit, and a replay
   reproduces.
9. **The movement course.** A sandbox lot (M7.6) with a flight, a ladder, a one-cell
   crawl gap, a 1 m wall with a held guard behind it and a 6 m drop; a fixture records a
   run through it with every move on the list.
10. **The fixtures.** Every fixture's hash moves once (content and the snapshot); each
    still records the run it is named for, checked the way G7.5 checked them.
11. **The Deck run.** CEOGG plays the course and Cold Storage's side route in Game Mode;
    every press arrives as its move in the run log; feel notes go in the gate.

## Order

1, 2, 7 (crouch on R3, so it can be felt from the first session), 3, 4, 3 again with a
held guard in the sandbox, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11.

## Out of scope

Agents crouching, sprinting or changing storeys (M9). Hearing footsteps (M9). Jump,
vault, mantle, prone, lean, swimming, ladders as their own piece kind. Animation (M8).

## Consequences for M8 (amendment proposed with this spec)

M8's precondition becomes G7.7 (it was G7.6), and claim 5's animation set is M7.7's list:
idle, walk, sprint, crouch (idle and moving), climb, fall, plus aim, fire, reload, hit and
death. "Run" is sprint. Footsteps (M8 claim 11) play from `actor.stepped`.
