## 3. Demo script (claim 13, CEOGG's Deck run)

From Steam, the **Gcity Sandbox** shortcut (the dev build, `build/linux-dev/gcity-dev.x86_64
-- --sandbox`; the release build has no sandbox and says so). In **Game Mode**, controller only:

1. The lot comes up; the HUD title reads `Gcity sandbox ... time x1`.
2. Build a wall: the creator's controls, as in M7.5 (D-pad moves the cursor, R1/L1 a level,
   Y the next piece, A places). A foundation row, a wall on it.
3. Start: the device, on the Creator app. R1: the **Spawn** app. D-pad left/right change
   its page, up/down choose, A does it at the cursor.
4. **agents** page: put the cursor behind the wall and spawn two guards with a g19 each
   (the content has pistols only; the spec's "rifles" wait for a rifle file, which then
   appears on this page with no code).
5. **trainer** page: god mode on. **inspect** page: sight lines on (or every overlay on).
6. Start to put the device down; walk into the guards' view. The HUD lists the overlays
   on; you take hits and lose nothing. The triggers fly you up and down (fly on, trainer page).
7. **ai** page: every guard frozen, then unfrozen; **time** page: hold, step one tick, x1/4, x1.
8. **record** page: save this session. The note names `user://fixtures/sandbox-<date-time>.json`.
9. Nothing more for CEOGG: `tools/check_sandbox_run.py --replay` (item 23) checks steps 1–8
   off the run log, imports the saved session and replays it twice to its hash.

Recorded for the gate: how long steps 2–8 took, the run log (`user://logs/runs/`), every
press arriving as its control, 1 % low with the overlays off and their cost with them on.

**The button map** (CEOGG, 2026-10-05, item 21), once per build CEOGG plays, a few minutes:
in Game Mode, from the **Gcity Creator** shortcut, press every button once with the device
down (D-pad, A, B, X, Y, L1, R1, Start, View, both sticks), then raise it and, in **each**
app (L1/R1 between them), press D-pad up/down/left/right, A and Y; B to put it down. Then on
the desktop `tools/check_buttons.py` lists every press and flags any that showed nothing or
arrived unbound. Repeat from the **Gcity Sandbox** shortcut for its Spawn app and triggers.
