Landing the rule with a constant of 2 would have stranded every guard in today's one-metre storeys until claim 6 rebuilds the sites, leaving the suite red across several sessions (CLAUDE.md §4). `body_cells` in `combat_profile` (schema: 1–4, required) lets the rule land with every profile at 1 — behaviour unchanged, proven by replay — and flip to 2 in the same change as the rebuilt sites.
