## 4. Debt and deviation log

| # | Item | Kind | Scheduled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The body's height is a profile field, not a constant.** The spec's assumptions put body height in a constant until a second body exists. Landing the rule with a constant of 2 would have stranded every guard in today's one-metre storeys until claim 6 rebuilds the sites, leaving the suite red across several sessions (CLAUDE.md §4). `body_cells` in `combat_profile` (schema: 1–4, required) lets the rule land with every profile at 1 — behaviour unchanged, proven by replay — and flip to 2 in the same change as the rebuilt sites. It is also the data-driven form a second body would need. | deviation from the spec's assumptions | claim 6 flips the profiles |
| 2 | **Raid tokens do not ask the body yet.** Tokens are not actors and cross one face per opening; what a two-cell opening means to them (one edge or two) is decided with the stacked openings in claim 6. | ordering | claim 6 |
| 3 | **Rising onto a step now checks the floor over the head.** `_climbs_onto` checked the cell above for ground and pieces but never a solid floor face between them; the body rule checks it at every height. No fixture moved (no wild step runs under a built floor in any of them). | correction | closed |
| 4 | **Nothing is built where a body is — for one-cell bodies too.** Until now a foundation could be placed on someone's feet, leaving them inside it (`MovementSystem` had a rule for "an actor put inside the ground stays there"). `BuildSystem.place` now refuses a cell piece in any live actor's body cells and a solid floor between them; `region.fill` refuses any body cell, not just the feet. No fixture moved (replayed 14:39–14:44). One test built over its own player (`test_vertical_movement` placed its foundation grid on the cell the player stood in); its player now starts off the build area. | behaviour change, claim 2 | closed |
| 5 | **The property's arenas each get a fresh sim.** In one sim, arenas side by side made the portal graph flood their joint bounding box and hit `MAX_REGION_CELLS`. Not a defect of the graph (a 400 m build would be refused the same way), but worth knowing for any test that builds far apart. | test design | closed |
